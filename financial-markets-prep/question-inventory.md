# Question & Worked-Example Inventory

Every worked example and practice/quiz question on the site, mapped to its source and the page
where its solution lives. Classification:

- **Class Material** — the problem (values + method) comes directly from the supplied notes.
- **Extra Practice** — an original exam-style question built on a method taught in class.

Each lesson additionally ends with an **interactive MCQ quiz** (10–13 questions). Mock test and
practice bank are listed at the end.
