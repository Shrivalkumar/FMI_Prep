### Extraction notes / limitations
- The decks were PowerPoint files exported to PDF, so some slides contained **chart/diagram images with no selectable text** (e.g. break-even charts in the Speed Run, the growth-comparison bar chart on `Session 9_Dividend_Growth_Model` slide 10, beta-estimation scatter visuals). The underlying numbers were recovered from adjacent tables and worked examples and are reproduced in the lessons; the decorative charts themselves were re-created as clean HTML/tables rather than copied.
- `Session 11` slide for the second FCFE example had one partially-cut figure in the source table ("17,?"); that single ambiguous cell was **not** invented — the FCFF/FCFE method is taught in full using the complete ABC Manufacturing example instead.
- No slide content was silently dropped. Where a chart could not be read as text it is noted above.
